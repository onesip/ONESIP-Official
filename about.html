Our story

Built around
one simple idea.

Great drinks should be easy to enjoy — on ordinary days as much as special ones. OneSip brings together food science, engineering and a love of tea to make every cup feel considered, consistent and approachable.

For everyone, for every moment.

Happy or tired, studying or catching up, hot summer day or quiet winter afternoon — there should always be a OneSip that fits.

Not from traditional foodservice — and that became our strength.

OneSip was created by two founders with backgrounds in science and engineering. Instead of beginning with restaurant habits, we began with questions: What makes tea taste stable? How do ingredients behave? Where does inconsistency come from? How can a drink be easier to make without compromising the result?

That way of thinking shaped the brand from the beginning. We focus on ingredients, recipe control, food safety, automation and repeatable processes — then turn all of that behind-the-scenes thinking into something simple for the customer: a drink that tastes good and feels easy to choose.

Our philosophy

Good tea should belong in real life.

We are not interested in making every cup feel complicated. We care about quality, balance, consistency and value — so the drink can fit naturally into your day.

“A cup should not depend on who happens to be making it. Good flavour can be thoughtful, standardised and still feel human.”

One menu, many moods.

Our menu moves between creamy milk tea, bright fruit tea, deep matcha, light tea lattes and fusion coffee. Some cups are comforting; some are refreshing; some are intentionally playful.

The common thread is the same: tea and flavour should stay clear, textures should have a reason to be there, and the final drink should be easy to come back to.
