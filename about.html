ABOUT US

Tea can be playful
and precise.

OneSip is a Rotterdam tea brand built for everyday life: easy to enjoy, visually calm, full of flavour and designed around better consistency from cup to cup.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Good drinks should feel easy.

You should not need a special occasion for a great drink. OneSip is about creating small moments of pleasure — a fresh matcha before class, a fruit tea on a warm afternoon, a milk tea with friends, or a quick pickup on a busy day.

We care about flavour first, but we also care about the things guests do not always see: repeatable recipes, thoughtful product design and a smoother ordering experience.

BRAND STORY

For everyone,
for every moment.

OneSip began with a question: could a modern tea shop be both creative and disciplined? We wanted the fun of bubble tea without relying on randomness, and the efficiency of a system without losing warmth.

That thinking shaped the brand. Matcha, fruit tea, milk tea, fresh lemon drinks and coffee can all sit under one roof when the common thread is clear: flavour, consistency and an easy guest experience.

Today, OneSip continues to grow from Rotterdam with the same idea at its core — make good drinks easier to enjoy, and make every everyday moment feel a little better.
